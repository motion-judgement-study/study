Human motion study

  • You will compare pairs of short 3D animations of a person, shown side by side: Left and Right.

  • Part 1: pick the one that looks more like a real person naturally doing this.

Part 1 of 2 · Natural movement

  • Pick the motion that looks more like a real person naturally doing what is described: smooth, balanced, physically plausible, and doing the described actions.
  • Exact timing comes in Part 2.

Part 2 of 2 · Timing, not naturalness

  • Now a timeline is added. Coloured bars mark when each action is asked for; the white line follows playback. During a bar, the requested body part turns the bar's colour.
  • Pick the motion that does each action inside its bar, with the right body part, and nothing extra outside the bars. Ignore smoothness here.
  • Some requests start with a movement such as walks backward. That part has no bar.

Example: both jump inside the jump bar, but Right also punches outside the punch bar, so Left is better.

Judge timing only, not how natural the motion looks.
